How the Engine Works

01
Vector Space Encoding

Your selected tags are converted into a binary feature vector across all known genres.

02
Cosine Similarity (40%)

Measures directional alignment between your preference vector and each item's tag vector.

03
Jaccard Similarity (35%)

Calculates tag overlap ratio — intersection ÷ union — for precision matching.

04
Rating Boost (15%)

High-quality items are boosted proportionally by their normalized community rating.

05
Type Preference (10%)

Items matching your selected content types receive a full bonus; others are penalized.
